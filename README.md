# Swiggy-Food-Delivery-Data-Analysis-Dashboard
This project analyzes 3,060 cleaned FY 2025 Swiggy food delivery orders (60 duplicate records removed) across 10 Indian cities (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, and Lucknow) and 10 food categories in alignment with the SapphireIQ analytics brief.

Executive Summary & EDA Findings
Usin zag Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn, the Swiggy food delivery dataset (3,060 orders across 10 Indian cities and 10 food categories) was cleaned, analyzed, and compiled into Swiggy_Food_Delivery_Analysis_2025.xlsx.  
Overall Scale & Basket Value: Total Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) is ₹18,37,564 with an Average Order Value (AOV) of ₹600.51 and Net Realized Revenue of ₹18,63,910 (Order_Value - Discount + Delivery_Fee).
Delivery & Fulfillment Performance: Average delivery time is 40.6 minutes, order fulfillment is 90.0%, and average customer satisfaction (CSAT) is 4.14 / 5.0. Ratings drop noticeably when delivery times exceed 45–60 minutes, particularly for temperature- and texture-sensitive categories such as Pizza, Burgers, and South Indian.
City & Category Dynamics: Tier-1 metros (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai) generate the highest order volume and GMV, while Pizza, Biryani, North Indian, and Continental lead in basket size.
Peak Ordering Windows: Demand peaks during Lunch (12:00–15:00) and Dinner (19:00–23:00), with higher order values on weekends (Friday–Sunday).

Executive_Dashboard: Formula-driven KPI cards, order status breakdown, 10-city performance summary, GMV bar chart, and order status pie chart.
Order_Customer_Patterns (Objective 1): Food category order volume, share, GMV, AOV, discounts, and ratings; payment method shares and cancellation rates; top 15 restaurant chains; and an AOV comparison chart.
Delivery_Performance (Objective 2): Delivery SLA buckets (Fast <30m, Standard 30–45m, Delayed 45–60m, Critical Delay >60m), distance band logistics and fee analysis, conditional formatting, and a CSAT chart.
City_Category_Matrix (Objective 3): Dynamic SUMIFS City × Food Category GMV cross-matrix and a rating resilience comparison (≤45m vs. >55m) across food categories.
Temporal_Trends (Objective 4): Day-part demand breakdown (led by Dinner 19:00–23:00 with 1,172 orders and Lunch 11:00–15:00 with 826 orders), day-of-week and weekend trends, a day-part chart, and prioritized operational recommendations.
Order_Data: Cleaned 2025 order-level records with dynamic Excel formulas for Net_Order_Value, Total_Customer_Payable, Delivery_Speed_kmph, Day_Part, and Delivery_SLA_Bucket, plus frozen headers, filters, and status validation.
